A Decades-Long Pursuit

The story began in January 2007 when Keri Chandler was found shot to death in her home in Creve Coeur, Missouri. Her estranged husband, Dana Chandler, quickly became the prime suspect. After years of investigation and a protracted legal process, Chandler was eventually convicted of first-degree murder in 2012. However, this was far from the final chapter.

The conviction was later overturned in 2016 by the Missouri Court of Appeals, citing prosecutorial misconduct. This decision sent shockwaves through the victim's family and the legal community, paving the way for a new trial. The subsequent years saw intense legal maneuvering, as both the defense and prosecution prepared for another showdown, with public interest remaining keenly focused on the outcome.
