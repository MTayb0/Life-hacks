Stop Porch Pirates: How to Protect Your Online Deliveries

2026-11-21 10:27 UTC · 💡 How-To

That satisfying 'ding' of a delivery notification quickly turns to dread when you open your door to an empty porch. Porch piracy, the theft of packages left at residences, has become an all too common headache for online shoppers. With e-commerce continuing to boom, these 'pirates' see easy targets in unattended parcels. But you don't have to be a victim! Here's how to safeguard your eagerly awaited deliveries.

Track Your Packages Relentlessly

The simplest defense is knowledge. Most major carriers (USPS, FedEx, UPS, Amazon) offer robust tracking services. Sign up for email or text alerts so you know exactly when your package is out for delivery and when it's supposed to arrive. This allows you to plan to be home or arrange for someone else to retrieve it.

Require a Signature

For high-value items, consider opting for a 'signature required' delivery. While it might mean a trip to the carrier's local office if you miss the delivery, it guarantees your package won't be left unattended. Check with the retailer or carrier about this option during checkout.

Utilize Delivery Lockers or Pickup Points

Many carriers and retailers now offer secure locker services or allow you to pick up packages at designated retail locations. Amazon Lockers, FedEx Office, and UPS Access Points are excellent options if you live in an area prone to theft or if you're frequently away from home. Simply have your packages shipped directly to one of these secure locations.

Install Security Cameras

Smart doorbells and security cameras are powerful deterrents. Just the visible presence of a camera can make a would-be thief think twice. If a theft does occur, the footage can be invaluable for identifying the culprit and assisting law enforcement.

Ship to Work or a Trusted Neighbor

If your workplace allows it, having packages delivered to your office can be a reliable solution, especially for smaller items. Alternatively, coordinate with a trusted neighbor who is often home and ask if they wouldn't mind accepting deliveries for you. Just be sure to return the favor!

Provide Specific Delivery Instructions

When ordering online, look for the option to add delivery instructions. You can ask the driver to place the package in a less visible spot, like behind a planter, next to a side door, or inside an unlocked gate. While not foolproof, it makes your package less obvious to opportunistic thieves.

What If a Package Is Still Stolen?

If despite your best efforts a package goes missing, don't despair. First, contact the seller or carrier immediately. Many have policies in place for lost or stolen items. If necessary, file a police report, especially if you have camera footage. Your credit card company might also offer purchase protection.

By taking a few proactive steps, you can significantly reduce your risk of becoming a victim of porch piracy and enjoy the convenience of online shopping with peace of mind.
